Appeal a Horizon Parking parking charge

Where to send your appeal, how long you have, and what happens if Horizon Parking says no. For Parking Charge Notices on private land in the UK.

Write a Horizon Parking appeal letter

Not legal advice, and not connected to Horizon Parking. This is general information and a template letter. It does not assess your case and no outcome is guaranteed.

Where to send it

Online: https://horizonparking.co.uk/appeal-a-parking-charge

The online form needs the Parking Charge reference number exactly as printed. Horizon says references with an "HP" or "HZ" prefix must include it.

By post: Appeals Department, Horizon Parking Ltd, Finitor House, 2 Hanbury Road, Chelmsford, Essex, CM1 3AE (from Horizon's own FAQ). Keep proof of posting.

This link was taken from Horizon Parking's own website on 11 October 2026 and the address on 17 November 2026. If your notice gives a different address or website, use the one on the notice.

How long you have

Appeal within 28 days of receiving the notice. Under the private parking sector single Code of Practice, the operator must reply within 28 days, or acknowledge your appeal and say when it will decide (clause 8.4.1).

If Horizon Parking rejects your appeal

Horizon's FAQ says it is a member of the British Parking Association and its Approved Operator Scheme, and that POPLA will not accept an appeal until you have appealed to Horizon first. It says a POPLA appeal must be made within 28 days of Horizon's decision, and that the reduced amount is no longer available if the POPLA appeal fails. For Scotland and Northern Ireland the FAQ says only the driver can appeal to POPLA.

The independent appeal is free for you, and enforcement must be suspended while it is decided (clause 8.4.7).

How Horizon Parking appeals went at POPLA

POPLA's 2025 annual report covers independent appeals between 1 October 2024 and 30 September 2025. For Horizon Parking in England and Wales (listed as "Horizon Parking Ltd (EW)"):

  • 2,470 appeals were decided: 314 allowed (12.7%) and 2,156 refused.
  • A further 1,427 appeals were not contested and closed without a decision. POPLA counts appeals dropped by the operator and by the motorist together in this figure; across all operators, 34,502 of 39,522 were dropped by the operator.

Across all operators, POPLA allowed 14,578 of 67,680 decided appeals (21.5%). These are past results across every kind of case, not a prediction for yours.

What to put in the letter

Pick the one ground that matches what happened and give the facts and evidence for it. The letter builder covers five:

  • The vehicle left within the grace or consideration period (clauses 5.1 and 5.2, Annex B).
  • The notice to keeper arrived more than 14 days after parking, with nothing left on the windscreen (England and Wales; Protection of Freedoms Act 2012, Schedule 4).
  • The tariff was paid but one character of the registration was mistyped (clause 6.3, Annex F).
  • Signs were missing, hidden or unreadable (clause 3.1, Annex A).
  • The payment machine or app was not working (Annex F.1(i)).

As registered keeper you do not have to say who was driving. Only state things that are true, and keep a copy of everything you send.

If you get a letter of claim or a court claim form

Do not ignore it. Both have strict deadlines, and an unanswered claim form leads to a county court judgment. Citizens Advice, law centres and Support Through Court offer free help.
